title: About EthioFoodCast
summary: What the tool does, who it is for, what it is not, and who built it.
---

EthioFoodCast helps field teams and programme staff in Ethiopia decide which farming households to visit first for a food-security assessment. It turns a short interview into a risk score, explains the main reasons behind it, and helps plan visits when there are more households than a team can reach.

## What it does

- **Scores one household.** A 14-question interview about the household, its land and crops, its livestock and its livelihood, plus the survey area where it lives, gives a score between 0 and 1 and a risk band: Low, Moderate or High.
- **Scores a list.** A spreadsheet of households can be uploaded and returned as a ranked list, highest risk first.
- **Plans visits.** The planning page shows how many households a team can expect to reach and how many food-insecure households a given number of visits is likely to find.
- **Shows the bigger picture.** Overview, regional, household profile and survey area pages summarise what the model found across the test households.

## Who it is for

The tool is designed for people who plan or carry out household visits: programme officers, field coordinators, enumerators and analysts working on food security and social protection. It is also meant to show researchers and reviewers exactly how the model was built and tested, including where it falls short.

## What it is not

- **Not an eligibility decision.** A score ranks households for a visit. Whether a household receives support must be decided by people, using a proper assessment.
- **Not a current forecast.** The model learned from surveys carried out in 2018/19 and 2021/22. It describes the patterns in those years, not today's conditions.
- **Not an official product.** EthioFoodCast is an independent research project. It is not endorsed by the Government of Ethiopia, the World Bank or any agency whose data or publications it uses.
